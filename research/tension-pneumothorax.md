# Tension pneumothorax (trauma / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/trauma/tension-pneumothorax.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

## The gap that matters

**No canine study of needle or finger decompression by non-veterinarians was found.** Canine evidence retrieved is iatrogenic pneumothorax after nasogastric tube misplacement (Odunayo 2023, 13 dogs; Hanchate 2025, 6 dogs) and a traumatic pneumomediastinum series (Klainbart 2024, 52 dogs and cats). Needle-decompression evidence is human (Robitaille-Fortin 2021 systematic review; Kaserer 2017, effective in 3 of 17) and swine (Martin 2012, 58% failure; Holcomb 2009, needle as good as chest tube in a swine model).

## What this means for the app

Human rules (needle longer than 7 cm, site choice) are anatomy-specific. The dog's thorax differs, and the framework itself lists tube thoracostomy and needle thoracostomy as separate procedure modules. The module states only what canine data support and marks the rest `TODO`.

## Not retrieved
