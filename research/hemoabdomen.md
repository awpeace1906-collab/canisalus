# Hemoabdomen (medical / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/medical/hemoabdomen.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

**Evidence:** Schick 2022 (systematic review, 1,150 dogs); Aronsohn 2009 (60 dogs); Pintar 2003 (39 dogs); Millar 2021 (197 dogs, premature death after benign histology; and 106 dogs, ultrasound cavitation); Stewart 2020 (prospective, 40 dogs); Menard 2023 (132 dogs, owner survey).

**Nuance for reviewers:** older series were gloomy (hemangiosarcoma-heavy); the prospective series found 95% survived to discharge and 37.5% were benign. Median survival with surgery 213 days vs 39 with palliative care, but the surgical benefit was lost in malignant cases (Menard 2023).

**Not retrieved:** prehospital care (fluids, pressure targets, autotransfusion, transport priority); traumatic hemoabdomen; pericardial effusion (separate module, not yet researched).
