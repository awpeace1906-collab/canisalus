# Smoke inhalation, carbon monoxide and cyanide (medical and tox-common / reversible)

Modules: `content/modules/medical/smoke-inhalation.json` and `content/modules/tox-common/smoke-co-and-cyanide.json`. Drafts. Not reviewed.

**Evidence:** Vigh 2024 (veterinary review, JAAHA); Dorfelt 2014 (9 dogs, 13 cats, German); Robertson 2026 (3 dogs, high-flow nasal cannula oxygen); Breen 1995 (canine experimental CO and cyanide model). Human context: Lawson-Smith 2011, Dries 2013.

**Key contrast:** oxygen saturation can read falsely normal with carbon monoxide (Vigh 2024). In the canine model, cyanide, not carbon monoxide alone, caused the severe cardiovascular depression (Breen 1995).

**Not retrieved:** any canine cyanide antidote or hyperbaric oxygen data; airway and intubation criteria in dogs; corticosteroid and bronchodilator evidence.

**Questions:** Vet: intubation triggers and CO-oximetry availability at each tier. Physician: hydroxocobalamin is a human antidote; should the app say nothing about it for dogs?
