# Snake envenomation (medical / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/medical/snake-envenomation.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

## Evidence retrieved (abstracts)

Carotenuto 2021 (282 dogs, crotalid, three antivenoms); Day 2024 (240 dogs and 98 cats, eastern brown snake); Padula 2017 (eastern brown snake, 16 dogs and cats); Padula 2020 (red-bellied black snake, 17 dogs); Lyon 2022 (in vitro thromboelastography, rattlesnake venom); Maduwage 2014 (human VICC review, context only).

## Key point for the app: region matters

The retrieved literature is North American pit vipers and Australian elapids, which produce different syndromes. **A region setting is needed** so a module does not show the wrong snake. No retrieved source covers other regions.

## What the sources support

See the module. Notably: crotalid survival was 96.1% and did not differ between three antivenoms (infusion reactions were higher with the IgG product); in eastern brown snake bites dogs show neurotoxicity plus coagulopathy where humans show mainly coagulopathy.

## Antivenom amounts

Day 2024 reports that more than 4000 units (of the antivenom used there) did not add survival benefit. **Not entered anywhere**; unit definitions differ by product.

## Not retrieved

Field first aid in dogs (pressure bandage, immobilization, tourniquet, and what to avoid); coral snake, cottonmouth and copperhead specifics; timing of antivenom; pain control; other regions.
