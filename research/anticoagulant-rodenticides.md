# Anticoagulant rodenticides (tox-common / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/tox-common/anticoagulant-rodenticides.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

## Evidence retrieved (abstracts)

Dartencet 2026 (58 dogs and cats, IV lecithin-solubilised vitamin K1); Mooney 2020 (4 dogs, IV vitamin K1, 2 suspected anaphylactoid reactions); Agostini 2025 (mixed-micelle IV phytomenadione vs blood products); Robben 1998 (21 dogs, plasma toxin levels); King 2015 (human LAAR review, context only).

## Study doses (not entered)

Dartencet 2026: 5 mg/kg IV. A 2026 Russian veterinary journal abstract (Lesnikova) reports parenteral phytomenadione at 2 to 5 mg/kg followed by 7 days or more. Human LAAR maintenance in King 2015 is 100 mg/day orally with courses averaging 168 days. **Human figures must never be used for a dog; canine dose and duration need a veterinary source.**

## Conflict to flag

IV vitamin K1 caused suspected anaphylactoid reactions in 2 of 4 dogs (Mooney 2020) but no adverse effects in 58 animals (Dartencet 2026) or in the mixed-micelle cohort (Agostini 2025). The products differ (formulation), which may explain it. Reviewer decision.

## Not retrieved

Guideline or consensus statement; canine treatment duration for long-acting agents; when to use plasma; oral vitamin K1 in dogs; bromethalin and cholecalciferol (separate modules).

## Questions for reviewers

Vet: route and duration; how to word monitoring (rebound coagulopathy after stopping). Physician: what a human clinician might wrongly copy from human superwarfarin management.
