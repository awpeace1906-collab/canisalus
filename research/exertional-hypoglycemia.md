# Hypoglycemia (exertional hypoglycemia stub): research dossier

**Status:** draft, one source (the 2026 RECOVER first-aid guideline, read in full text from PubMed Central). No source specific to exertional hypoglycemia. Nothing approved.

| # | Source | Design | Used for |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Thawley 2026, JVECC 36 Suppl 1:S3-S35 (PMID 42640821, PMC13505872) | GRADE guideline, section 3.1 (FA-06) | Four graded recommendations on feeding, buccal carbohydrate, seizing animals, rescuer risk |

**Evidence base the guideline relied on:** one small trial in 40 parvovirus dogs (not designed to test oral sugar), and a study in 12 healthy dogs (Holt). Everything else is human data. Quality: very low.

**Doses recorded for the vet reviewer only (not in the module):** Holt study gave 1 mL/kg of corn syrup or 50% dextrose buccally to 12 healthy dogs; mean glucose rose about 45 to 49 mg/dL within 15 minutes. The guideline itself states no dose and says dose-response studies are lacking. No cat data.

**Gaps:** exertional hypoglycemia in working dogs; glucose thresholds; sugar amount.
