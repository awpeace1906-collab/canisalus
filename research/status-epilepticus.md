## What the sources support

Recorded in the module. Highlights: benzodiazepines are first line; intranasal midazolam stopped seizures in 14/20 vs 3/15 for rectal diazepam (2017); IN vs IV midazolam 76% vs 61%, IN faster once IV placement time is counted (2019); sedation and ataxia are expected; owners preferred intranasal.

## Study doses as reported (reference only, not recommendations)

Intranasal and intravenous midazolam were compared at 0.2 mg/kg each (2019). A small Indian study (Parin 2024, abstract only) reports IV lorazepam 0.2 mg/kg and diazepam 0.5 mg/kg. **None is entered in the app.**

## Not retrieved
