# Cocaine (tox-duty / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/tox-duty/cocaine.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

**Evidence:** Thomas 2014 (19 dogs, urine-positive, one hospital); Oster 2023 (review). A 2024 JAMA Network Open research letter on cat and dog cocaine or methamphetamine exposure trends appeared in search; its abstract has no clinical content, so it is not used.

**Weakness:** one small hospital series selected on a positive urine test. No decontamination or antidote evidence retrieved.

**Questions:** Vet: field measures and monitoring window; Physician: differences from human cocaine chest pain and hypertension management that must not carry over.
