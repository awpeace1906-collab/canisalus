# Hypothermia (trauma / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/trauma/hypothermia.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

## Evidence retrieved

One veterinary review is the anchor: Brodeur 2017, *J Vet Emerg Crit Care* (hypothermia and targeted temperature management in cats and dogs). Everything else retrieved is human (Jose 2024, 3 million trauma patients), rodent (Burggraf 2019), swine (Palmerton 2026; Blakeman 2026), or experimental canine cardiac-arrest models (Wu 2006; Nozari 2004) that study **induced** hypothermia as a therapy, not accidental hypothermia in a patient. Only the first two of those are used, and only as labelled context.

## Conflicts to flag

Rewarming rate evidence points both ways: slow rewarming improved survival in a rat shock model (Burggraf 2019) while a swine trauma study supported rapid rewarming (Palmerton 2026), and a Japanese human cohort linked faster rewarming to better survival (Hara 2025). **None is used in the module.**

## Not retrieved

Canine target temperatures, rewarming rates, hypothermia thresholds by severity, and field techniques (blankets, warm fluids). The framework lists hypothermia under trauma; no retrieved source ties it to canine trauma specifically.
