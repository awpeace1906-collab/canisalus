# Weight estimation: research dossier

**Status:** draft, two sources (abstract level). Nothing approved.

| # | Source | Design | Used for |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Blystone 2023, JAVMA (PMID 37495225) | Prospective, 272 dogs in an ER | Owner estimates within 10%: 68%; attendings 41%; house officers 35%; technicians 35%; experience did not help |
| 2 | Wolf and Drobatz 2022, JAVMA (PMID 36563068) | Prospective, 101 dogs and 28 cats | Underestimation rises with weight; long coat hurts accuracy; 32% within 10% |

**Seen, not used:** Yam 2017 (Vet Sci, owners; 48% of owners within 10%, a different setting and lower than Blystone), and body-composition papers (Leung 2018, Witzel 2014), which are about fat, not weight estimation.

**Gaps:** working-dog and field methods, accuracy margin to display, whether a kennel weight counts as measured.
