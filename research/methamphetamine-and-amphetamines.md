# Methamphetamine and amphetamines (tox-duty / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/tox-duty/methamphetamine-and-amphetamines.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

**Evidence:** Oster 2023 (review of recreational drugs in dogs and cats; abstract only); case reports only for the drugs themselves (Pei 2014 methamphetamine; Harris 2022 amphetamine and lipid emulsion; Martindale 2024 lisdexamfetamine, fatal; Smith 2020 amphetamine rhabdomyolysis); Markert 2023 (lipid emulsion in 313 dogs and 100 cats, mixed toxicants).

**Reported figures kept out:** Harris 2022 states oral median lethal dose ranges for amphetamines in dogs of about 9 to 11 mg/kg up to 20 to 27 mg/kg, and the case ingested about 12.3 mg/kg. Not entered.

**Weakness:** no case series for methamphetamine in dogs; treatment statements are case-level.

**Not retrieved:** sedation, cooling and blood-pressure strategy; how ILE compares with standard care; MDMA and other drugs.

**Questions:** Vet: what should Tier 0 to 2 do (cooling, seizure control)? Physician: which human stimulant-toxicity habits (for example beta-blockers) should be explicitly not applied without veterinary input?
