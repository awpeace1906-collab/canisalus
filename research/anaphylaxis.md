# Anaphylaxis (medical / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/medical/anaphylaxis.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

**Rank-1 source exists but was not read:** RECOVER First Aid Guidelines: Acute Allergy and Anaphylaxis in Dogs and Cats (Burkitt-Creedon 2026, JVECC). Abstract: 6 PICO questions, 12 treatment recommendations, 7 on low or very low quality evidence and 5 expert opinion, with an algorithm. **Full text needed for the drug, dose and route.**

**Canine vs human contrast (from Turner 2021, 232 dogs):** vasoconstrictive shock 49% vs vasodilatory 13%; gallbladder wall edema on ultrasound in 59% of dogs scanned; dermatological signs absent in about a quarter.

**Caution on outcomes:** Kadowaki 2026 (49 dogs, 91.8% survival) and Smith 2020 (67 dogs, 85%) are retrospective and cannot show whether epinephrine timing changes survival. Fosset 2023 found no association of glucocorticoids with recovery.

**Not retrieved:** dose and route of epinephrine; observation period; biphasic reactions.
