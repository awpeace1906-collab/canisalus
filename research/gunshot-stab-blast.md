# Gunshot and stab wounds, blast injury: research dossier

**Status:** draft, abstract level. Nothing approved. No dose or technique given.

| # | Source | Design | Used for |
|---|--------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Miller 2018, Mil Med (PMID 29547926) | 92 deployed MWD deaths, 2001-2013 | GSW 31.5%, blast 26.1%, heat stress 9.8% of external causes; injuries 77.2% of deaths |
| 2 | Edwards 2021, JVECC (PMID 34014602) | 193 MWD, 35 OpK9, 26,099 civilian dog trauma cases | Penetrating trauma most frequent; explosions 22.3% of MWD injuries, only in MWDs; GSW and knife injuries more common in MWDs |
| 3 | Capak 2016, Top Companion Anim Med (PMID 28317616) | 166 dogs with projectiles | Thoracic injury 14.4x fatality risk; airgun 62% |
| 4 | Olsen 2014, JAVMA (PMID 25285934) | 37 cats and dogs | Working breeds 78%; ATT score predicts outcome |
| 5 | Schrock 2021, VCOT (PMID 34875717) | 97 animals, 137 gunshot fractures | Poor outcome in 20 of 37 with follow-up; soft tissue injury matters |
| 6 | Thawley 2026 RECOVER first aid (PMID 42640821) | GRADE guideline | Tourniquet recommendations |

**Not retrieved:** stab wounds in dogs; blast injury patterns in dogs; field management of chest and abdominal penetration; the 160th SOAR CASEVAC survey (Redman 2018) mentions 8 MWDs among 227 patients but is a human casualty study and is not cited.
**Bias note:** deployed-dog data describe combat and are not the same population as handler-owned working dogs at home.
