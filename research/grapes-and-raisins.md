# Grapes and raisins (tox-common / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/tox-common/grapes-and-raisins.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

## Evidence retrieved (abstracts)

Reich 2019 (139 dogs); Eubig 2005 (43 dogs with renal signs, ASPCA APCC); Schweighauser 2020 (15 dogs, neurological signs with AKI); Wegenast 2022 (cream of tartar and tamarinds, tartaric acid); Downs 2023 (dried vs fresh); Tancredi 2025 (viewpoint); Cook 2025 (probenecid pharmacokinetics in 6 beagles).

## The important nuance

Early series (Eubig 2005) describe dogs that had already developed renal failure: 53% survived. A larger, later series of all dogs with known ingestion (Reich 2019) found AKI in 6.7% with data and 138 of 139 surviving. The two are not contradictory: they sample different populations. **The module states both.** Reviewers to decide how to word risk for a responder who does not know the amount.

## Study doses (not entered)

Cook 2025 gave probenecid 50 mg/kg orally to healthy beagles for pharmacokinetics only. It is not a treatment recommendation and the toxin-exposure trial has not been done.

## Not retrieved

Any consensus statement; a safe-amount threshold (none is established in the abstracts); fluid therapy and monitoring duration.

## Questions for reviewers

Vet: monitoring window and whether decontamination is advised for unknown amounts. Physician: no human analogue; confirm that the "no specific antidote" wording is acceptable.
