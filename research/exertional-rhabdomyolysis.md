# Exertional rhabdomyolysis (medical / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/medical/exertional-rhabdomyolysis.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

## Evidence retrieved

Canine: Devall 2018 (sled dogs, myoglobin and CK, 5 dogs with rhabdomyolysis, 12 controls) and Spinella 2021 (23 search and rescue dogs, CK and AST after 20 minutes). Heatstroke complication link: Bruchim 2017 (already cited in the heat-stroke module). Human context: Scalco 2016, Rojas-Valverde 2020, Vangstad 2026 (136 patients; outpatient care safe when admission creatinine normal). Equine papers appeared and were not used.

## Honest summary

The canine evidence is two small studies. Neither tests a treatment. Treatment statements in the human literature (hydration, outpatient management) must not be carried across.
