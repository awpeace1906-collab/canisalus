# Cannabis and THC (tox-duty / reversible)

Module: `content/modules/tox-duty/cannabis-and-thc.json`. Draft. Not reviewed.

**Evidence:** Binagia 2024 (223 dogs, 100% survival); Loewen 2024 (38 dogs, plasma cannabinoids); Amissah 2022 (veterinarian survey; first author corrected from Khokhar after PubMed check); FitzGerald 2013 (review); Lauinger 2021 (15 dogs, human feces source); Brutlag 2018 (review, abstract only, not cited).

**Reported figures kept out of the module:** FitzGerald 2013 states a minimum lethal oral THC dose for dogs above 3 g/kg, with deaths seen from concentrated medical-grade THC butter. Second-hand review figure; reviewer decision.

**Not used, and why:** a 6-dog case report of transmucosal CBD as treatment (Marsigliano 2024) is too small and uncontrolled; a beagle pharmacokinetic study of a THC:CBD extract (Chicoine 2020) is not a toxicosis study.

**Conflict on urine screens:** FitzGerald says human urine screens are unreliable in dogs; Lauinger found all 8 screened dogs positive; Binagia says a negative screen must not rule cannabis out.

**Not retrieved:** guideline text; decontamination advice; the framework's claim that edibles often also contain xylitol or chocolate.

**Questions:** Vet: any role for CBD, lipid emulsion or induced emesis? Physician: legal and evidentiary handling of a positive result in a K9 unit (a jurisdiction question).
