# Finding the nearest 24/7 vet ED

Module id: `finding-the-nearest-24-7-vet-ed`  |  Domain: handoff  |  Status: **draft**  |  Kind: clinical

> DRAFT for review. Nothing here is approved. Do not use for patient care.

## Why this matters

The nearest open veterinary ER may not be the nearest clinic, and deciding where to go before an emergency saves time; the app supports two ways to find one (this is product guidance, not a clinical claim, and needs reviewer sign-off).

## Claims that differ from human practice

- There is no national dispatch that routes a dog to an open veterinary ER the way 911 routes a person, so the handler must know or find the clinic.

## Recognition

- Local missions: record the 24/7 emergency vets for each area you work on the K9 profile (Vet ER screen, or K9 profiles then Edit), and note which accept working dogs.
- Unfamiliar territory: use the Vet ER screen's search, which opens your phone's maps app. The app does not read your location or look anything up itself.

## Management

- Call before you drive. Opening hours shown in maps or saved earlier can be wrong, so confirm they are open and can see your dog.
- Saved entries show when they were last verified and flag any older than 180 days (a product default); re-verify by phone and update the date.
- Directions are offered only for saved clinics that have an address, so a name alone cannot send you to the wrong town.
- Hand over with the Handoff summary so the receiving clinic gets the dog's weight (and whether estimated), MDR1 status and what was done.
